# Market, originality and brand

## Known comparators (research leads, not comprehensive audit)
- Time Tracker Pro (Autodesk marketplace): automatic Revit tracking / idle detection.
- Project Tracker (Autodesk marketplace): time by project/file/view/task.
- INFINITY Suite Time Summary / Advanced Analytics: Revit time summaries and per-file analytics; UI referenced from user's screenshots.
- ManicTime: automatic desktop time tracking and Autodesk-related integrations.

## Originality assessment
Generic time tracking is not original. Cross-app tracking and manager reporting already exist. The proposed combination of observed CAD/BIM activity, actual-vs-billed hours, cost realization and historical scope-based estimating is a differentiation hypothesis, NOT proof of novelty or patentability.

## Naming
- Working name: **BIMSpend** (not legally cleared).
- User checked Namecheap and shared screenshot showing `bimspend.com` available for A$17.22 promotional first-year price at that time. This is not proof of purchase, registration, trademark availability or future pricing.
- BIM Pulse and BIMetrics already used commercially; rejected.
- ASIC business names, IP Australia trademarks, WIPO international trademarks, .com.au and .ai availability still need official verification.
