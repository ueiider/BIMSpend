## Key insight
Three distinct metrics: (1) measured/estimated ACTUAL time, (2) APPROVED/BILLED time, (3) internal COST. `Effective realized rate = invoiced revenue / actual hours`. Difference between hours × rate and invoiced revenue is unrealized charge-out value, not automatically accounting loss. Quoting may be informed by historical scope/complexity and actual effort, but cannot be derived reliably from hours alone.
