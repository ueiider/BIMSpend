# Product vision — BIMSpend (exploration only)

**Status: EXPLORATION ONLY. No product architecture, feature, brand rights, pricing, or implementation is approved.**

## Problem the concept addresses

Architecture, engineering, and BIM companies deliver projects against fees, but effort data is fragmented:
- Designers lose time manually reporting hours.
- Time is recorded per application/file, not aggregated by project and employee.
- Managers cannot easily distinguish actual effort from billed effort and discretionary discounts.
- Quoting future work relies on subjective experience and thin historical benchmarks.

## Working product hypothesis

A low-friction, subscription-based platform that observes work sessions across Autodesk applications (initially Revit, potentially AutoCAD, Navisworks, Civil 3D, and others), aggregates time by project and employee through a background Windows desktop application with optional application connectors, and exposes project cost monitoring, actual-vs-billed comparison, historical analytics, and future-estimate assistance through a management dashboard reachable from another computer.

**Core user requirement:** Designers install once and have no routine interaction (no daily login, no manual project selection, no timers).

## Personas (unapproved)

- Designer: zero routine interaction; transparent monitoring and corrections/appeals process.
- Project manager: view time, project budgets, cost drivers, scope changes.
- Business owner: portfolio costs, capacity, realization, historical estimates.
- Admin: deployment, user mapping, permissions, organization/project identifiers, retention.

## What is deliberately undecided

- Primary job-to-be-done: accurate project hours, billing recovery, or quote accuracy?
- Capture scope: all applications or only supported CAD/BIM tools?
- Signals for "active working time" without invasive surveillance?
- Model-to-commercial-project mapping across disciplines and renames.
- Cloud vs local-first architecture; per-seat vs team vs enterprise licensing.
- What is permitted under employment law, customer contracts, and Autodesk terms.

## Differentiation hypothesis (not proof of novelty)

Automatic time tracking already exists (Time Tracker Pro, Project Tracker, INFINITY, ManicTime). The proposed combination of observed CAD/BIM activity, actual-vs-billed hours, cost realization, and historical scope-based estimating is a differentiation hypothesis. No originality, patent, or freedom-to-operate claim is established.

## Brand

Working name: **BIMSpend** (not legally cleared). `bimspend.com` was observed offered for sale at A$17.22 first year (time-dependent); not proof of purchase, registration, trademark availability, or future pricing.
